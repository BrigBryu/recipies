# Pecan Pie

## Ingredients

- 1 unbaked pie crust
- 1 large egg
- 1 tbsp milk or heavy cream
- 2 1/2 cups shelled pecans
- 3 large eggs
- 1 cup dark corn syrup
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Prepare 1 unbaked pie crust in a 9 inch pie dish, brush the edges with 1 large egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk or heavy cream, and chill the crust briefly if you want a neater edge.
2. Heat the oven to 350 F.
3. Roughly chop 2 1/2 cups pecans and spread them evenly in the pie crust.
4. Whisk 3 large eggs, 1 cup dark corn syrup, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, 1/4 cup melted butter, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon together until combined.
5. Pour the filling over the pecans.
6. Bake the pie for 50 to 55 minutes, shielding the crust edges after about 20 minutes if they brown too quickly.
7. Cool the pie completely on a wire rack before slicing.

## Serve With

- Whipped cream or ice cream.

## Notes

- No need to pre-bake the crust.
- Store leftovers at room temperature for 1 to 2 days or in the refrigerator for 4 to 5 days.
