# Soy Milk

## Ingredients

- 1 cup soybeans
- 6 cups water, divided
- 5 to 6 tbsp sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp vanilla, optional

## Instructions

1. Rinse 1 cup soybeans and soak them overnight.
2. Rinse the soaked soybeans again and remove any loose skins.
3. Blend the soybeans with 2 cups water for about 1 minute using the spread setting on the blender.
4. Add 4 more cups water to the blender mixture.
5. Use the white seal for the pressure cooker and cook the soy mixture for 4 minutes, then leave it in the cooker for 20 extra minutes.
6. Strain the soy milk through cheesecloth, then strain it a second time to be sure.
7. Stir 5 to 6 tbsp sugar and a pinch of salt into the strained soy milk, and add 1 tsp vanilla if using.

## Serve With

- Drink warm or chilled.
