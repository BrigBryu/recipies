# Cherry Pie

## Ingredients

- 312 g all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 225 g unsalted butter, cold and cubed
- 120 g plain yogurt, cold
- Ice water, as needed
- 1 kg dark frozen cherries
- 135 g granulated sugar
- 28 g cornstarch
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp almond extract
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 28 g butter, cut small
- 1 large egg
- 1 tbsp water for egg wash

## Instructions

1. Whisk 312 g all-purpose flour, 1 tsp sugar, and 3/4 tsp salt together, then cut in 225 g cold cubed butter until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces left.
2. Stir in 120 g cold yogurt and add ice water a teaspoon at a time until the dough just holds together without becoming wet or sticky.
3. Pat the dough into a rough rectangle, roll it into a 10 inch rectangle, fold it like a letter, turn it 90 degrees, and repeat once more.
4. Divide the dough into 2 discs, wrap them, and chill them for at least 2 hours.
5. Combine 1 kg frozen cherries, 135 g granulated sugar, 28 g cornstarch, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp almond extract, and 1/2 tsp salt in a large saucepan.
6. Cook the cherry mixture over low heat until the cherries thaw and release liquid, then raise the heat to medium and stir until the mixture bubbles and turns thick and glossy, about 2 minutes once bubbling.
7. Cool the filling completely.
8. Heat the oven to 425 F, roll 1 dough disc into a 12 inch circle, and fit it into a 9 inch pie dish.
9. Spoon the cooled cherry filling into the crust and dot the top with 28 g butter.
10. Roll the second dough disc into another 12 inch circle and use it as a top crust or cut it into strips for a lattice, then seal the edges.
11. Whisk 1 large egg with 1 tbsp water and brush it over the top crust.
12. Bake the pie at 425 F for 15 minutes, then lower the oven to 350 F and bake for 60 more minutes, until the crust is deep golden and the filling bubbles in thick slow bubbles.
13. Cool the pie for at least 4 hours before slicing.

## Serve With

- Whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.

## Notes

- Dark sweet frozen cherries work best.
- Store-bought crust works if you want a shortcut.
