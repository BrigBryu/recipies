# Pesto

## Ingredients

- 2 cups packed basil leaves
- Spinach or parsley, optional for part of the basil
- 1/4 cup pine nuts
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 2 garlic cloves, optional
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil

## Instructions

1. Add 2 cups packed basil leaves, 1/4 cup pine nuts, 1/2 cup grated parmesan, the juice of 1 lemon, 1/8 tsp salt, and 2 garlic cloves if using to a blender.
2. Pulse the blender while slowly pouring in 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil.
3. Blend for about 30 seconds more, until the pesto is fully combined.

## Serve With

- Pasta, sandwiches, or pizza.

## Notes

- If you do not have enough basil, replace part of it with spinach or parsley.
