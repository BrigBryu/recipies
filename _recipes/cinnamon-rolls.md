# Cinnamon Rolls

## Ingredients

- 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour, plus more as needed
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup whole milk
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 1/4 tsp instant yeast
- 1 large egg
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 2/3 cup confectioners' sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Whisk 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour, 1/4 cup granulated sugar, and 1/2 tsp salt together in a large bowl.
2. Warm 3/4 cup whole milk with 3 tbsp unsalted butter until the butter melts and the mixture is warm but not hot, then whisk in 2 1/4 tsp instant yeast.
3. Pour the milk mixture into the dry ingredients, add 1 large egg, and mix until a soft dough forms.
4. Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for 3 to 5 minutes until smooth, adding a little more flour only if needed.
5. Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover it loosely, and let it rest for 10 minutes.
6. Roll the dough into a 14 by 8 inch rectangle, spread 3 tbsp softened butter over it, and sprinkle on 1/3 cup brown sugar mixed with 1 tbsp ground cinnamon.
7. Roll the dough into a log, cut it into 10 to 12 rolls, and arrange the rolls in a greased 9 or 10 inch pan.
8. Cover the pan and let the rolls rise in a warm spot for 60 to 90 minutes, until doubled.
9. Heat the oven to 375 F and bake the rolls for 24 to 27 minutes, tenting loosely with foil if they brown too quickly.
10. Beat 4 oz softened cream cheese, 2 tbsp softened butter, 2/3 cup confectioners' sugar, and 1 tsp vanilla extract until smooth.
11. Spread the icing over the warm rolls and serve right away.

## Serve With

- Coffee or milk.

## Notes

- This is a one-rise recipe, which makes it good for yeast beginners.
- The dough can be assembled the night before and chilled.
