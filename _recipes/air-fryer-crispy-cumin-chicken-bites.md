# Air Fryer Crispy Cumin Chicken Bites

## Ingredients

- 887 g chicken, tightly cubed small
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lemon juice
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp sugar
- 2 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp baking soda, optional
- 1 cup cornstarch
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp crushed cumin seeds, optional

## Instructions

1. Cut 887 g chicken into small tight cubes.
2. Mix the chicken with 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lemon juice, 1 tbsp oil, 2 tsp salt, 2 tsp sugar, 2 1/2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp black pepper, and 1/2 tsp baking soda if using.
3. Marinate the chicken for 20 to 40 minutes.
4. In a separate bowl, mix 1 cup cornstarch, 1/4 cup flour, 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp garlic powder, and 1 tsp baking powder.
5. Toss the marinated chicken in the coating until every piece looks dry, dusty, and fully covered.
6. Add 2 tbsp oil and toss again until the coating looks sandy and clumpy, not wet.
7. Preheat the air fryer to 400 F.
8. Spray or oil the basket, then arrange the chicken in a single layer without crowding it and lightly oil the tops if needed.
9. Air fry the chicken for 8 minutes, shake or flip it, then air fry it for 8 more minutes.
10. While the chicken is still hot, toss it with 1 tbsp ground cumin, 1 1/2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp sugar, 3/4 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and 1 tsp crushed cumin seeds if using.

## Serve With

- Rice, cucumbers, or a simple vegetable side.

## Notes

- The final dry cumin toss is what gives it the takeout-style flavor.
- Small cubes are important for crunch.
- Do not crowd the air fryer or the chicken will steam.
- This worked best at 400 F for 8 minutes plus 8 minutes.
- For 1/3 of the final spice batch, use 1 tsp ground cumin, 1/2 tsp paprika, 1/4 tsp sugar, 1/4 tsp salt, 1/8 tsp black pepper, and 1/8 tsp garlic powder.
