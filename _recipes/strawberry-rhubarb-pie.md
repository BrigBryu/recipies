# Strawberry Rhubarb Pie

## Ingredients

- 1 double pie crust
- 3 cups sliced rhubarb
- 2 1/2 cups chopped strawberries
- 1/3 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp orange juice
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, cut small
- 1 large egg
- 1 tbsp milk
- Coarse sugar, optional

## Instructions

1. Prepare 1 double pie crust and keep it chilled.
2. Stir 3 cups sliced rhubarb, 2 1/2 cups chopped strawberries, 1/3 cup brown sugar, 1/3 cup granulated sugar, 1/4 cup cornstarch, 1/4 tsp salt, 1 tbsp orange juice, and 1/2 tsp vanilla extract together in a large bowl.
3. Heat the oven to 400 F.
4. Roll 1 crust into a 12 inch circle and fit it into a 9 inch pie dish.
5. Spoon the fruit filling into the crust, leaving excess liquid behind in the bowl, and dot the filling with 2 tbsp unsalted butter.
6. Roll the second crust into a 12 inch circle and either weave it into a lattice or use it as a full top crust with vents.
7. Brush the top crust with 1 large egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk and sprinkle with coarse sugar if using.
8. Bake the pie on a baking sheet for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 350 F and bake for 30 to 35 minutes more, shielding the crust if needed, until the filling bubbles around the edges.
9. Cool the pie for at least 3 hours before serving.

## Serve With

- Vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.

## Notes

- This pie slices better if made a day ahead.
