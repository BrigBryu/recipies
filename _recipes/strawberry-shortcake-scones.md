# Strawberry Shortcake Scones

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 6 tbsp chilled unsalted butter, cubed
- 10 small strawberries, hulled and finely diced
- 3/4 cup half and half
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 1/4 cup half and half
- 1/2 tsp vanilla

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400 F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Whisk 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tbsp baking powder, 3 tbsp granulated sugar, and 1/2 tsp salt together in a large bowl.
3. Cut 6 tbsp chilled butter into the flour mixture until it looks crumbly, then toss in 10 diced strawberries and coat them lightly with the flour mixture.
4. Add 3/4 cup half and half and fold gently just until the mixture comes together into a soft, wet dough.
5. Turn the dough onto a floured surface, pat it into a 1 inch thick circle or rectangle, and cut it into 6 to 8 triangles.
6. Bake the scones for 16 to 18 minutes, until cooked through and lightly golden.
7. Cool the scones on a rack for about 10 minutes.
8. Whisk 2 cups powdered sugar, 1/4 cup half and half, and 1/2 tsp vanilla together into a glaze, then dip or drizzle the tops of the scones.

## Serve With

- Tea, coffee, or extra berries.

## Notes

- Half and half is equal parts whole milk and heavy cream.
- Different berries work here too, though some bleed more color than strawberries.
