# Loaded Scrambled Eggs

## Ingredients

- Carrot
- Zucchini
- Onion
- Baby potato
- Eggs
- Salt
- Garlic powder
- Soy sauce
- Mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Dice carrot, zucchini, onion, and baby potato and saute the vegetables until tender.
2. Beat the eggs with salt, garlic powder, and soy sauce.
3. Pour the seasoned eggs into the pan with the vegetables and scramble until almost done.
4. Add mozzarella cheese on top near the end and let it melt.

## Serve With

- Toast or rice.

## Notes

- The vegetable and egg amounts are flexible in your notes.
