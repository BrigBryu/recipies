# Spring Rolls

## Ingredients

- Peanut butter
- Soy sauce
- Hoisin sauce
- Rice vinegar
- Sesame oil
- Carrot
- Cucumber
- Lettuce
- Crab stick
- Fine rice vermicelli
- Tofu
- Oyster sauce
- Rice paper wraps

## Instructions

1. Make a dipping sauce with peanut butter, soy sauce, hoisin sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame oil.
2. Boil water and cook the fine rice vermicelli for 2 to 3 minutes, then rinse the noodles in cold water.
3. Crumble tofu and fry it with soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil until seasoned and lightly browned.
4. Prepare carrot, cucumber, lettuce, and crab stick for filling.
5. Soften the rice paper wraps one at a time, then fill each wrap with vermicelli, vegetables, crab stick, and seasoned tofu.
6. Roll the wraps tightly and serve with the dipping sauce.

## Serve With

- Extra sauce on the side.

## Notes

- The sauce and filling amounts are flexible.
