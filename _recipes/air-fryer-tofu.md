# Air Fryer Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 block extra-firm tofu, frozen and thawed or fresh and pressed
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- Paprika, to taste
- Soy sauce, to taste
- Garlic powder, to taste
- Pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- Oil spray for the tray

## Instructions

1. Drain 1 block extra-firm tofu and press out as much water as possible, whether the tofu is fresh or frozen and thawed.
2. Mix 1/2 cup olive oil with paprika, soy sauce, garlic powder, and pepper to taste.
3. Cut the tofu into pieces, put it in a large bowl, and stir in the seasoned oil mixture.
4. Add 2 tbsp cornstarch and toss again.
5. Spray the air fryer tray with oil and air fry the tofu for 12 minutes.

## Serve With

- Rice bowls, noodles, or dipping sauce.

## Notes

- The seasoning amounts are intentionally flexible in your notes.
