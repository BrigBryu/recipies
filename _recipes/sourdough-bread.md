# Sourdough Bread

## Ingredients

- Active starter from a feeding the night before
- 120 g active starter
- 1 1/3 cups warm filtered water
- 4 cups plus 2 tbsp bread flour
- 2 tsp salt
- Pecans, optional
- Craisins, optional
- Flour for shaping

## Instructions

1. Feed the starter the night before so it is bubbly and doubled by the next day, then start around 10 am.
2. Mix 120 g active starter, 1 1/3 cups warm filtered water, 4 cups plus 2 tbsp bread flour, and 2 tsp salt in a large bowl until you have a shaggy dough.
3. Cover the bowl with a damp towel and let the dough rest on the counter for 1 hour.
4. Wet your hands and do 4 stretch and folds, turning the bowl 90 degrees between each fold, then cover and rest the dough for 20 minutes.
5. Repeat that stretch-and-fold step 4 times total, with the rounds at about 11:20, 11:40, 12:00, and 12:20.
6. Transfer the dough to a fresh bowl, cover it, and leave it on the counter for 6 hours.
7. Turn the dough out onto a clean work surface with wet hands, add pecans and craisins if using, and shape it by flouring one side, flipping it, pinching the corners up, flipping it again, and tightening it into a ball by scooting it toward you.
8. Dust the dough with flour, line a fresh bowl with a clean smooth towel, sprinkle flour into the bowl, and place the dough into the bowl with the smooth side down.
9. Sprinkle more flour over what is now the top of the loaf, cover it, and refrigerate for 12 hours or up to 36 hours.
10. Take the dough out while you preheat the oven with a cast iron pot inside to 450 F for 1 hour.
11. Sprinkle more flour on the loaf, place parchment paper over the bowl, flip the loaf out onto the parchment, and score the top.
12. Carefully lower the loaf into the hot pot, cover it, and bake at 450 F for 20 minutes.
13. Remove the lid and bake for 20 more minutes, or until the crust is golden brown.

## Serve With

- Butter, soup, cheese, or toast toppings.

## Notes

- The original video says 500 F, but your note says 450 F because your oven does not go higher.
- Be very careful handling the preheated cast iron pot.
