# Strawberry Sauce

## Ingredients

- 1 lb strawberries, rinsed, hulled, and thickly sliced
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Combine 1 lb sliced strawberries, 1/3 cup granulated sugar, and 1 tbsp lemon juice in a medium saucepan.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the strawberries release their juices.
3. Reduce the heat and simmer the sauce for 20 to 23 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens.
4. Cool the sauce to room temperature before using.

## Serve With

- Ice cream, pancakes, waffles, pie, cheesecake, or trifle.

## Notes

- The sauce thickens more as it cools and even more after refrigeration.
