# Tofu Patties (Ganmodoki)

## Ingredients

- Version 1: okara from 1 batch soy milk, 1 can tuna, 2 eggs, 1 carrot, 2 mushrooms, nori, oyster sauce, soy sauce, olive oil
- Version 2: 1 pack tofu, 1 carrot, edamame or mushrooms or seaweed paper, 1 egg, 2 tbsp cornstarch, salt or soy sauce

## Instructions

1. For Version 1, mix the okara from 1 batch of soy milk with 1 can tuna, 2 eggs, 1 chopped carrot, 2 chopped mushrooms, nori, oyster sauce, soy sauce, and a little olive oil, then air fry the patties in the Instapot.
2. For Version 2, squeeze the water out of 1 pack tofu using the beige tofu maker, or press the tofu for about 2 hours with weights if you want a better texture.
3. Shred 1 carrot as finely as possible and finely chop the edamame, mushrooms, or seaweed paper you want to use.
4. Blend the tofu and vegetables, return the mixture to a bowl, then add 1 egg and 2 tbsp cornstarch.
5. Season the tofu mixture with salt or soy sauce, mix well, then shape it into patties.
6. Fry the patties in hot oil on the stove or air fry them for 12 minutes.

## Serve With

- Rice, dipping sauce, or vegetables.

## Notes

- If the vegetables are not chopped finely enough, the patties can turn out crumbly.
