# Pan Fried Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 block tofu
- Olive oil
- Garlic
- Soy sauce
- Garlic flakes or onion flakes
- Water
- Spring onions, optional

## Instructions

1. Slice 1 block tofu into thin slices and pat the slices dry between 2 towels.
2. Heat the stove to level 8 and pour olive oil into a pan.
3. Fry the tofu in batches until browned.
4. Separately mix garlic, soy sauce, garlic flakes or onion flakes, and water.
5. Pour the sauce into the pan after all the tofu is cooked.
6. Add spring onions if you have them and toss the tofu in the sauce.

## Serve With

- Rice and vegetables.

## Notes

- Your notes do not give exact sauce amounts, so season this one by taste.
