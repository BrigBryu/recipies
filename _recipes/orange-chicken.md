# Orange Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, chopped
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp orange juice
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 orange, zested

## Instructions

1. Brown 1 1/2 lb chopped chicken thighs in a wide skillet with a little oil until cooked through.
2. Stir together 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp orange juice, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp cornstarch, 1 grated garlic clove, 1 tsp grated ginger, and the zest of 1 orange.
3. Pour the sauce into the skillet and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until glossy and lightly thickened.
4. Toss the chicken to coat and serve immediately.

## Serve With

Steamed rice and a quick green vegetable.

## Notes

- Add red pepper flakes if you want a little heat.
- Double the sauce if you like extra for rice.
