# Orange Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, chopped
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp orange juice
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 orange, zested

## Instructions

1. Brown the chicken in a wide skillet with a little oil until cooked through.
2. Stir together soy sauce, orange juice, vinegar, brown sugar, cornstarch, garlic, ginger, and orange zest.
3. Pour the sauce into the skillet and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until glossy and lightly thickened.
4. Toss to coat and serve immediately.

## Serve With

Steamed rice and a quick green vegetable.

## Notes

- Add red pepper flakes if you want a little heat.
- Double the sauce if you like extra for rice.
