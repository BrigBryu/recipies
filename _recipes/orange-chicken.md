# Orange Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 to 2 lb chicken breast, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 can mandarin oranges in syrup
- 1 small can mushrooms, drained
- 1 can diced tomatoes, drained
- 2 to 3 tbsp oil
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp sugar or honey
- 1 tbsp cornstarch or flour
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder or 2 minced garlic cloves
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Small pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Drain 1 can mandarin oranges, saving the syrup and setting the orange slices aside for later.
2. Stir the reserved mandarin syrup with 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice, 1/2 tsp sugar or honey, 1 tbsp cornstarch or flour, 1/2 tsp garlic powder or 2 minced garlic cloves, 1/4 tsp black pepper, and a small pinch of salt.
3. Heat 2 to 3 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium-high heat, then cook 1 1/2 to 2 lb chopped chicken for 6 to 8 minutes until the outside is no longer pink and the pieces start to brown.
4. Add 1 chopped onion and cook for 5 to 7 minutes until softened.
5. Add 1 small drained can mushrooms and about 1/2 can drained diced tomatoes, then cook for 3 to 5 minutes so some of the liquid cooks off.
6. Pour in the orange sauce and simmer for 3 to 6 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken.
7. Gently stir in the reserved mandarin orange slices and cook for 1 minute, just long enough to warm them through.

## Serve With

Rice, noodles, or tortillas.

## Notes

- If you have more tomatoes than you want, start with half the can.
- The sauce is intentionally flexible and works with either vinegar or lemon juice.
