# Apple Pie

## Ingredients

- 1 recipe double pie crust
- 2 1/4 lb Granny Smith apples, peeled and cored
- 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 8 tbsp unsalted butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp water for egg wash

## Instructions

1. Make 1 recipe double pie crust and chill it according to the crust instructions while you prepare the filling, then heat the oven to 425 F.
2. Melt 8 tbsp unsalted butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat, whisk in 3 tbsp all-purpose flour, and simmer for 1 minute while whisking constantly.
3. Whisk 1/4 cup water and 1 cup granulated sugar into the butter mixture, bring it to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer for 3 minutes while whisking frequently, then remove the sauce from the heat.
4. Thinly slice 2 1/4 lb apples, or about 7 cups, into a large bowl, sprinkle them with 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon, and toss to combine.
5. Pour the sauce over the apples and stir until the slices are coated.
6. Roll the bottom pie crust into a 12 inch circle on a floured surface, transfer it to a 9 inch pie plate, and mound the apple filling slightly in the center without getting filling on the edges.
7. Roll the second crust into an 11 inch round, cut it into 10 even strips, and weave the strips into a lattice over the filling.
8. Beat 1 egg with 1 tbsp water and brush the lattice with the egg wash.
9. Bake the pie at 425 F for 15 minutes, then reduce the oven to 350 F and bake for 45 more minutes, or until the apples are soft and the filling bubbles through the vents for at least 5 minutes.
10. Rest the pie at room temperature for 1 hour before serving.

## Serve With

- Vanilla ice cream.

## Notes

- Put a sheet of foil on the rack below the pie to catch drips.
- Granny Smith apples are the default, but Golden Delicious, Honeycrisp, Pink Lady, or a mix also work.
- Store covered at room temperature for up to 2 days or refrigerate for up to 4 days.
