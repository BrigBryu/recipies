# Classic Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 batch crispy tofu
- 2 to 3 green onions
- About 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- Large amount of spinach
- Sesame seeds, optional
- Sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Cook 1 batch crispy tofu and set it aside on a plate or in a bowl.
2. Chop 2 to 3 green onions and cook them in the same pan.
3. Add about 1 tsp minced garlic and 1 tbsp soy sauce.
4. Return the crispy tofu to the pan and add a large amount of spinach.
5. Stir in sesame seeds if using and finish with sesame oil just before removing the tofu from the heat.

## Serve With

- Rice.

## Notes

- This one builds directly on the crispy tofu method.
