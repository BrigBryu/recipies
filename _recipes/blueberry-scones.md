# Blueberry Scones

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for hands and work surface
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, frozen
- 1/2 cup heavy cream, plus 2 tbsp for brushing
- 1 large egg
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 heaping cup fresh blueberries
- Coarse sugar, optional
- Vanilla icing, optional

## Instructions

1. Whisk 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1/2 cup granulated sugar, 2 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp ground cinnamon, and 1/2 tsp salt together in a large bowl.
2. Grate 1/2 cup frozen unsalted butter into the flour mixture and work it in until you have pea-sized crumbs, then chill the bowl while you mix the wet ingredients.
3. Whisk 1/2 cup heavy cream, 1 large egg, and 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract together.
4. Drizzle the cream mixture over the dry ingredients, add 1 heaping cup blueberries, and mix until everything is moistened.
5. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface, shape it into a ball with floured hands, and press it into an 8 inch disc.
6. Cut the disc into 8 wedges, brush the tops with the remaining 2 tbsp heavy cream, and sprinkle with coarse sugar if using.
7. Chill the scones for at least 15 minutes while you heat the oven to 400 F and line a baking sheet.
8. Arrange the chilled scones 2 to 3 inches apart on the baking sheet and bake for 22 to 25 minutes, until golden brown around the edges and lightly browned on top.
9. Cool the scones briefly before adding vanilla icing if you want it.

## Serve With

- Tea, coffee, or extra berries.

## Notes

- Fresh blueberries work best.
- Very cold dough helps prevent over-spreading.
