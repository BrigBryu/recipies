# Caramelized Onions for Burgers

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 large yellow onions
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp water, wine, or beer

## Instructions

1. Melt 2 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter and 1 tbsp olive oil over medium-low heat.
2. Slice 2 large yellow onions, add them to the pan, and sprinkle them with 1/4 tsp salt.
3. Cook the onions uncovered over low heat for 45 to 60 minutes, stirring occasionally at first and more often toward the end, until they are golden to deep golden brown.
4. In the last few minutes, add 2 tbsp water, wine, or beer and scrape the pan bottom with a wooden spatula to deglaze it.

## Serve With

- Burgers, sandwiches, or veggie patties.

## Notes

- Low heat is the key to getting sweetness without burning.
