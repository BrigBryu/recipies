# Pie Crust

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 sticks chilled unsalted butter
- 1/4 to 1/2 cup cold water

## Instructions

1. Combine 2 1/2 cups flour, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tsp sugar.
2. Use the large blender on the dough setting to mix in 2 sticks chilled unsalted butter until the mixture is coarsely combined.
3. Add 1/4 to 1/2 cup cold water and mix just until the dough comes together.
4. Wrap the dough and refrigerate until ready to use, or freeze it for later.

## Serve With

- Use for sweet or savory pies.

## Notes

- Start with the smaller amount of water and add more only if needed.
