# Fried Rice

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- Oil
- Crab sticks, optional
- Day-old rice
- Frozen vegetables or edamame or other vegetables
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce, optional
- Water, as needed
- Sesame oil, optional

## Instructions

1. Fry 1 egg in a little oil.
2. Add cut up crab sticks if using.
3. When the egg is almost done, break it up and add the day-old rice.
4. Add frozen vegetables or edamame or whatever vegetables you have on hand.
5. Stir in 1 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp oyster sauce if using.
6. Add a little water if the rice seems dry.
7. Finish with sesame oil if you want it.

## Serve With

- Chili crisp or extra soy sauce.

## Notes

- This is a flexible clean-out-the-fridge recipe.
