# Strawberry Cream Cheese Tart

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 to 3 cups sliced strawberries
- 1/2 cup blueberries

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400 F.
2. Cream 1/2 cup softened butter and 1/2 cup sugar together, add 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, then mix in 1 cup flour until a dough forms.
3. Press the dough into the bottom of a greased 10 inch springform pan and bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until golden brown.
4. Cool the crust in the pan while you make the filling.
5. Beat 8 oz softened cream cheese with 1/4 cup powdered sugar until smooth, then beat in 1/4 cup heavy cream and 1 tsp vanilla extract until soft and fluffy.
6. Remove the cooled crust from the springform pan, spread the cream cheese filling over it, and arrange 2 to 3 cups sliced strawberries and 1/2 cup blueberries on top.
7. Refrigerate the tart until serving.

## Serve With

- More fresh berries.

## Notes

- Add the berries within a couple hours of serving if you want the neatest look.
