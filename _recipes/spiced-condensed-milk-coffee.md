# Spiced Condensed Milk Coffee

## Ingredients

- 1 strong cup hot coffee
- 2 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
- 1 pinch ground cardamom
- 1 pinch cinnamon
- Tiny pinch kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Brew the coffee a little stronger than usual.
2. Stir the condensed milk, cardamom, cinnamon, and salt into the hot coffee until fully dissolved.
3. Taste and adjust sweetness or spice.

## Serve With

Toast, a cookie, or absolutely nothing.

## Notes

- Works well over ice if you let the coffee cool first.
- A little vanilla is nice but optional.
