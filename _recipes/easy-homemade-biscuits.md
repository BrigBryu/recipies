# Easy Homemade Biscuits

## Ingredients

- 8 tbsp salted butter, divided
- 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for the work surface
- 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 tbsp white sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup milk

## Instructions

1. Cut 6 tbsp salted butter into small cubes and place them in the freezer while you prepare the rest of the dough.
2. Heat the oven to 425 F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat.
3. Mix 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp baking powder, 2 tbsp white sugar, and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl or food processor.
4. Add the frozen 6 tbsp butter cubes and cut or pulse them into the flour mixture until the butter pieces are no larger than peas and the mixture looks sandy.
5. Pour in 3/4 cup milk and stir or pulse just until a crumbly dough forms.
6. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface, press it to about 1 inch thick, fold it in half, and press it back to 1 inch thick, repeating that folding step 3 times total.
7. Cut the dough into biscuits with a floured biscuit cutter or glass, pressing straight down without twisting, then reshape scraps and cut more biscuits.
8. Place the biscuits on the baking sheet so they are just barely touching and bake for 12 to 14 minutes, until the tops are lightly golden.
9. Melt the remaining 2 tbsp butter and brush it over the tops before serving.

## Serve With

- Breakfast, dinner, or jam and honey.

## Notes

- Whole milk or 2% milk works best.
