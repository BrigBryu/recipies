# Roasted Pumpkin Seeds

## Ingredients

- 1 cup pumpkin seeds
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Set the Instapot to Bake and set the temperature to 325 F.
2. Toss 1 cup pumpkin seeds with 1 tbsp olive oil and salt to taste.
3. Spread the seasoned pumpkin seeds in the pot or baking insert and bake for 10 minutes.

## Serve With

- Eat warm as a snack or sprinkle over salads and soup.

## Notes

- Watch closely near the end if your machine runs hot.
