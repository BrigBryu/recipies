# Eggplant Tofu

## Ingredients

- 2 Asian eggplants
- Oil for shallow frying
- Tofu, cooked separately
- 1 onion
- Minced garlic
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp oyster sauce
- 2 tsp ketchup
- 1 tsp sugar

## Instructions

1. Cut 2 Asian eggplants lengthwise into several strips, then crosswise into smaller chunks.
2. Fill a pan about 1 cm deep with oil, heat it until the eggplant sizzles on contact, and fry the eggplant until cooked, then set it aside.
3. Cook the tofu separately and set it aside.
4. Chop 1 onion and fry it with some minced garlic.
5. Stir together 1 tsp soy sauce, 1 tsp oyster sauce, 2 tsp ketchup, and 1 tsp sugar, then add that sauce to the pan.
6. Add the eggplant back in and stir well.
7. Keep the tofu separate until serving if you prefer.

## Serve With

- Rice.

## Notes

- Your notes say you like to keep the tofu separate until it goes on the plate.
