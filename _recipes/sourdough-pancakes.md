# Sourdough Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sourdough starter
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 to 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp sugar
- Dash of salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 can creamed corn, optional

## Instructions

1. The night before, combine 1 cup sourdough starter, 1 1/2 cups flour, 2 eggs, 1 to 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp sugar, a dash of salt, and 1 cup milk.
2. Cover the batter and refrigerate it overnight.
3. In the morning, stir 1 tsp baking powder and 1/2 tsp baking soda into the batter.
4. If you want the corn version, fold 1 can creamed corn into the batter after adding the leaveners.
5. Cook scoops of batter on a hot greased skillet until browned on both sides.

## Serve With

- Maple syrup, butter, or fruit.
