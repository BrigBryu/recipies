# Crepes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour, sifted
- 1 tbsp melted butter
- A few drops of oil for the pan

## Instructions

1. Stir together 1 cup milk, 2 eggs, 1 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt.
2. Sift in 3/4 cup all-purpose flour and stir well.
3. Stir in 1 tbsp melted butter.
4. Heat a non-stick pan on stove setting 8 and add a few drops of oil.
5. Pour in enough batter to cover the pan evenly, spread it around a little, and leave it alone until the crepe is firm all the way through.
6. Flip the crepe and cook the second side briefly.

## Serve With

- Apple pie filling, banana and Nutella, or Malaysian pancake-style fillings.

## Notes

- Filling ideas from your notes: apple with sugar and cinnamon, banana and Nutella, or creamed corn with butter, sugar, and ground peanuts.
