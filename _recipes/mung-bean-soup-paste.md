# Mung Bean Soup Paste

## Ingredients

- 1 14 oz bag mung beans, or red beans or adzuki beans
- Coconut milk powder, to taste
- Sugar, to taste
- Water to about double the height of the beans in the pot

## Instructions

1. Add 1 14 oz bag mung beans to the Ninja pot and pour in enough water to reach about double the height of the beans.
2. Set the Ninja pot to pressure cook for 1 hour.
3. Let the pressure release naturally for 20 more minutes.
4. Stir in coconut milk powder and sugar to taste until the beans turn into the soup or paste consistency you want.

## Serve With

- Warm as a sweet soup or chilled as a thicker paste.

## Notes

- You can use mung beans, red beans, or adzuki beans.
- The final sweetness and thickness are meant to be adjusted by taste.
