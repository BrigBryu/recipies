# Spongy Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 package tofu

## Instructions

1. Freeze 1 package tofu overnight.
2. Move the frozen tofu to the main part of the fridge the next day.
3. Use the tofu on the third day, or thaw it faster by soaking it in boiled water.
4. Cook the thawed tofu using the crispy tofu method if you want your usual finish.

## Serve With

- Any sauce that benefits from a chewy tofu texture.

## Notes

- Soaking in boiled water works, but your notes say it takes a long time.
