# Instant Pot Mango Sticky Rice

## Ingredients

- 1 cup glutinous rice
- 1 cup cold water for the pot
- 2/3 cup cold water
- 2/3 cup coconut milk
- 1/4 cup plus 1 tbsp sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 1 mango, peeled and thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp coconut flakes
- 1/2 cup coconut milk
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 pinches salt
- 2 tbsp coconut milk for the cornstarch slurry

## Instructions

1. Pour 1 cup cold water into the Instant Pot and set a steamer basket inside.
2. In a stainless steel bowl, combine 1 cup glutinous rice with 2/3 cup cold water, making sure the rice is submerged, then place the bowl on the steamer basket.
3. Pressure cook the rice on high pressure for 12 minutes, then let it naturally release for 12 minutes.
4. While the rice cooks, warm 2/3 cup coconut milk with 1/4 cup plus 1 tbsp sugar and a pinch of salt over medium-low heat until the sugar dissolves.
5. Pour the sweetened coconut milk into the cooked rice, fluff gently, and let it sit for 30 to 60 minutes so the rice absorbs the mixture.
6. Toast 2 tbsp coconut flakes in a skillet over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown.
7. Stir 1 tbsp cornstarch into 2 tbsp coconut milk to make a slurry.
8. Simmer 1/2 cup coconut milk with 2 tbsp sugar and 2 pinches salt, then slowly stir in the cornstarch slurry until the sauce thickens.
9. Slice 1 mango and serve it with scoops of the sticky rice, drizzled with coconut sauce and topped with the toasted coconut flakes.

## Serve With

- Extra coconut sauce.

## Notes

- Use ripe mangoes.
- Use canned unsweetened coconut milk, not the beverage kind.
