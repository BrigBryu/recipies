# Crispy Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 block tofu
- Cornstarch, for coating
- 2 to 3 tbsp oil

## Instructions

1. Cut 1 block tofu into 1 inch cubes or slices and squeeze out the excess water.
2. Coat the tofu on all sides with cornstarch.
3. Heat 2 to 3 tbsp oil in a pan on setting 9.
4. Add the tofu in a single layer so the pieces are flat on the pan and not touching, working in batches if needed.
5. Sear each side fully before turning, or at least the top and bottom if that is your usual method.
6. Move the tofu around as needed so it does not stick, and cook until golden brown.

## Serve With

- Use as the base for other tofu dishes.

## Notes

- Your notes mention using chopsticks in one hand and a fork in the other to keep the tofu moving.
