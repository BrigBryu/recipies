# Smoothie

## Ingredients

- 1 cup almond milk
- 1/2 avocado, or 1 whole avocado
- 1 banana
- 1 cup frozen blueberries
- Honey, to taste

## Instructions

1. Add 1 cup almond milk, 1/2 avocado or 1 whole avocado, 1 banana, and 1 cup frozen blueberries to the blender.
2. Blend until smooth, then add honey to taste and blend again if needed.

## Serve With

- Drink immediately.

## Notes

- Use a whole avocado for a thicker smoothie.
