# Malaysian Pancake

## Ingredients

- 100 g sourdough starter
- 50 g rice flour
- 50 g glutinous rice flour
- 100 g water
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp milk
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp water
- Crushed peanuts, for topping
- Sugar, for topping
- Creamed corn, for topping
- Butter, for topping

## Instructions

1. The night before, mix 100 g sourdough starter, 50 g rice flour, 50 g glutinous rice flour, and 100 g water, then let it sit overnight.
2. In the morning, stir in 2 eggs, 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp brown sugar, 1/4 tsp salt, 1 tbsp oil, and 2 tbsp milk.
3. Mix 1/2 tsp baking soda with 1 tsp water, then stir that mixture into the batter.
4. Heat a non-stick pan on level 8 without adding oil or butter.
5. Pour a scoop of batter into the pan and spread it evenly and thinly, including up the sides of the pan.
6. Cook the pancake on only 1 side until the whole surface looks dry.
7. Add butter, crushed peanuts, sugar, and creamed corn as desired, then fold the pancake in half and serve.

## Serve With

- Butter, peanuts, sugar, and creamed corn.
