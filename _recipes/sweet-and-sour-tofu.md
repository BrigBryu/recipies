# Sweet and Sour Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 batch crispy tofu
- 4 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 2 to 3 tbsp water
- 1 to 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- Vegetables or mushrooms, optional
- Canned pineapple, optional

## Instructions

1. Cook 1 batch crispy tofu and set it aside.
2. Stir together 4 tbsp tomato ketchup, 2 to 3 tbsp water, 1 to 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp minced garlic, and 1 tsp cornstarch.
3. If using vegetables or mushrooms, saute them in the pan after the tofu is done.
4. Add the sauce to the pan and heat it until simmering.
5. Add canned pineapple when you add the sauce if you want it.
6. Plate rice and the crispy tofu, then pour the hot sauce over the top.

## Serve With

- Rice.

## Notes

- Your note says the rice vinegar can easily overpower everything, so be careful with it.
