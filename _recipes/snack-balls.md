# Snack Balls

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/4 cup protein powder
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 3 tbsp water
- 2 tbsp cacao powder
- 1 cup homemade granola

## Instructions

1. Mix 1/2 cup peanut butter, 1/4 cup protein powder, 2 tbsp maple syrup, 3 tbsp water, and 2 tbsp cacao powder until smooth.
2. Stir 1 cup homemade granola into the mixture.
3. Roll the mixture into small balls and chill them if you want them firmer.

## Serve With

- Store cold or room temperature for snacks.

## Notes

- Add a little more water only if the mixture feels too dry to roll.
