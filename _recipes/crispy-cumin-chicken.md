# Crispy Cumin Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 small yellow onion, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Toss the chicken with oil, salt, cumin, paprika, chili flakes, garlic powder, and cornstarch.
2. Spread in the air fryer basket and cook at 400 F for 14 to 18 minutes, shaking halfway through, until browned and crisp at the edges.
3. Meanwhile, cook the onion in a skillet over medium heat with a little oil until soft and caramelized.
4. Taste and add another pinch of salt or cumin if needed.

## Serve With

White rice and caramelized onions.

## Notes

- Great with cucumber salad on the side.
- If using breast meat, check for doneness a few minutes early.
