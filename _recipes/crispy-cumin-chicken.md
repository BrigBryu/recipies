# Crispy Cumin Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 small yellow onion, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Toss 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs with 2 tbsp neutral oil, 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, 2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp chili flakes, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and 2 tbsp cornstarch.
2. Spread the seasoned chicken in the air fryer basket and cook at 400 F for 14 to 18 minutes, shaking halfway through, until browned and crisp at the edges.
3. Meanwhile, cook 1 small sliced yellow onion in a skillet over medium heat with a small splash of oil until soft and caramelized.
4. Taste and add another pinch of salt or cumin if needed.

## Serve With

White rice and caramelized onions.

## Notes

- Great with cucumber salad on the side.
- If using breast meat, check for doneness a few minutes early.
