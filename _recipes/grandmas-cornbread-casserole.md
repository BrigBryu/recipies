# Grandma's Cornbread Casserole

## Ingredients

- 1 box Jiffy corn bread mix
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 can creamed corn
- 1 can kernel corn, drained
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 stick melted butter

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350 F.
2. Mix 1 box Jiffy corn bread mix, 1 cup sour cream, 1 can creamed corn, 1 drained can kernel corn, 2 eggs, and 1/2 stick melted butter.
3. Pour the lumpy mixture into a baking dish.
4. Bake at 350 F for about 45 minutes, depending on thickness.
5. If you double the recipe, bake it for about 1 hour or longer.

## Serve With

- Holiday meals or roast chicken.

## Notes

- The mixture will be lumpy.
