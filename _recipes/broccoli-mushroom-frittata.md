# Broccoli Mushroom Frittata

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 2 tsp Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp olive oil or melted coconut oil
- 1 cup frozen broccoli, heated in the microwave
- Oyster sauce, to taste
- Mushrooms, to taste
- Onions, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350 F and lightly oil the square blue glass pan with 1 tsp olive oil or melted coconut oil.
2. Beat 4 eggs with 2 tsp Greek yogurt in a bowl.
3. Stir 1 cup microwaved broccoli, mushrooms to taste, onions to taste, and oyster sauce to taste into the egg mixture.
4. Pour the mixture into the prepared pan and bake for 20 to 30 minutes, until the center is fully cooked.

## Serve With

- Toast, fruit, or a simple salad.

## Notes

- The amount of mushrooms, onions, and oyster sauce is flexible.
