# Sourdough Pizza

## Ingredients

- Sourdough pizza dough, using the Brundy sourdough recipe
- Olive oil
- Onion
- Mozzarella
- Feta or blue cheese or Gorgonzola
- Walnuts
- Pesto, optional

## Instructions

1. Prepare sourdough pizza dough using the Brundy sourdough recipe.
2. Stretch the dough, brush it with olive oil, and top it with onion, mozzarella, feta or blue cheese or Gorgonzola, and walnuts.
3. Bake the pizza until the crust is cooked and the cheese is melted.
4. Add pesto after cooking if you want it.

## Serve With

- Salad or roasted vegetables.

## Notes

- The exact dough quantity and bake time depend on the sourdough dough batch you use.
