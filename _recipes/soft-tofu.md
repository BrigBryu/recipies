# Soft Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 package soft tofu
- Sauce and other cooked ingredients of your choice

## Instructions

1. Cook your sauce and any other ingredients completely before adding 1 package soft tofu.
2. Cut up the soft tofu and add it to the pan only long enough to heat it through, about 1 minute.

## Serve With

- Rice or noodles.

## Notes

- The tofu is already cooked in the package, so it only needs gentle reheating.
