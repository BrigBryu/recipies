# Granola

## Ingredients

- 5 cups oats
- 1 cup chopped almonds
- 1/2 cup maple syrup or honey
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- Chia seeds, optional
- Sunflower seeds, optional
- Pumpkin seeds, optional
- Coconut flakes, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 300 F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Mix 5 cups oats, 1 cup chopped almonds, 1/2 cup maple syrup or honey, and 1/2 cup olive oil, plus any chia seeds, sunflower seeds, pumpkin seeds, or coconut flakes you want to add.
3. Spread the granola mixture on the prepared baking sheet.
4. Bake at 300 F for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring once if needed for even browning.

## Serve With

- Milk, yogurt, or fruit.

## Notes

- You can chop the almonds yourself if you like the texture better.
