# Creamy Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 onion
- 3 carrots
- Mushrooms
- 1 pack tofu
- Canned corn, optional
- Oyster sauce
- Salt
- Black pepper, optional
- Half and half
- Water
- Grated cheese
- Cornstarch

## Instructions

1. Chop 1 onion, 3 carrots, and some mushrooms.
2. Fry the onion in a skillet first, then add the carrots and mushrooms.
3. Transfer the vegetables to the electric pot and set it to sear or saute.
4. Mash 1 pack tofu and add it to the pot.
5. Add canned corn if using, then season with oyster sauce, salt, and optional black pepper.
6. Pour in half and half and water, then add grated cheese.
7. Stir the soup until it simmers.
8. Add cornstarch a little at a time, starting with about 1 tsp, until the soup thickens the way you want.
9. Taste and add more of whatever it needs until you are happy with it.

## Serve With

- Bread or crackers.

## Notes
