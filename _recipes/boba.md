# Boba

## Ingredients

- Boba pearls
- Water for a full pot
- Sugar, to taste

## Instructions

1. Fill a pot with water and bring it to a full boil.
2. Add the boba pearls and lower the heat to stove setting 7 or 8.
3. Boil the boba for about 1 minute, then test 1 pearl to see if it is soft enough.
4. Scoop the boba into a bowl and dump out the old water.
5. Rinse the boba with cold water to wash off the starch, then pour off all but a little of the water.
6. Add sugar to taste and heat the boba just long enough for the sugar to melt.

## Serve With

- Milk tea or sweet drinks.

## Notes

- The exact cooking time depends on the kind of boba you are using.
