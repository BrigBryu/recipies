# Teriyaki Tofu

## Ingredients

- 1 batch crispy tofu
- Oyster sauce
- Soy sauce
- Hoisin sauce
- Sesame oil
- Shaoxing wine, optional
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- Water for thinning the cornstarch
- Vegetables or mushrooms, optional
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Cook 1 batch crispy tofu and set it aside.
2. Stir together oyster sauce, soy sauce, hoisin sauce, sesame oil, optional Shaoxing wine, 1 tsp minced garlic, and 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with a little water.
3. If using vegetables or mushrooms, saute them in the pan first.
4. Add the sauce to the pan and heat it until simmering.
5. Return the crispy tofu to the pan and toss it until the tofu is coated.
6. Sprinkle sesame seeds over the tofu before serving.

## Serve With

- Rice or noodles.

## Notes

- The sauce amounts are flexible in your notes.
