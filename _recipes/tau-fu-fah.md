# Tau Fu Fah

## Ingredients

- 4 cups homemade unsweetened soy milk
- Pandan extract, optional
- 1 tsp calcium sulfate
- 1/4 tsp calcium sulfate per 1 cup soy milk for single servings
- Water for steaming
- Brown sugar or rock sugar

## Instructions

1. Stir a little soy milk into 1 tsp calcium sulfate in a bowl to dissolve it.
2. Add the remaining 4 cups soy milk, plus pandan extract if using, and try not to jiggle the bowl after the soy milk goes in.
3. Steam the bowl for 10 minutes.
4. Let the tau fu fah cool for 10 minutes.
5. If the tau fu fah is not fully set, refrigerate it for a few hours.
6. Make a syrup with equal parts water and brown sugar or rock sugar, then spoon the syrup over the tau fu fah when serving.

## Serve With

- Warm syrup.

## Notes

- For single servings, use 1/4 tsp calcium sulfate for every 1 cup soy milk.
