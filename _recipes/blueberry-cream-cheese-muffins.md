# Blueberry Cream Cheese Muffins

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg white
- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups blueberries
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 375 F and line a muffin tin with paper liners.
2. Stir 1/4 cup vegetable oil and 4 tbsp melted butter together in a large bowl.
3. Whisk 1 cup granulated sugar, 1 large egg, and 1 large egg white into the oil mixture until combined.
4. Whisk 1/2 cup buttermilk and 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract into the wet ingredients.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tsp baking powder, and 1/2 tsp salt together.
6. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until mostly combined, then fold in 1 1/2 cups blueberries until the batter is just combined.
7. Stir 8 oz softened cream cheese with 1/3 cup granulated sugar and 1/2 tsp vanilla extract until smooth for the filling.
8. Stir 1/4 cup all-purpose flour, 1/4 cup brown sugar, and 1/8 tsp salt together, then drizzle in 2 tbsp melted butter and toss with a fork until clumpy for the streusel.
9. Fill 15 muffin cups about 2/3 full with batter, make a small crater in each one, and add about 2 tsp cream cheese filling to each muffin.
10. Top each muffin with a spoonful of remaining batter, gently swirl the batter and filling together with a knife, and sprinkle on the streusel.
11. Bake the muffins for about 25 minutes, until baked through, then cool them for about 10 minutes before serving.

## Serve With

- Coffee or tea.

## Notes

- Frozen blueberries work, but they can streak the batter purple.
- If baking the last 3 muffins as a second batch, start checking them around 17 minutes.
