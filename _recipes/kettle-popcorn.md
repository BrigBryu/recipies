# Kettle Popcorn

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup coconut oil
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/3 cup popcorn

## Instructions

1. Heat 1/4 cup coconut oil in a pot on stove setting 8 for 1 to 2 minutes.
2. Add 1/3 cup popcorn and 1/4 cup sugar, then put the lid on.
3. When the popcorn starts popping, turn the stove down to 7.
4. Shake the pot gently until the popping slows and the popcorn is done.

## Serve With

- Eat warm.

## Notes

- The measurements are approximate in your notes.
