# Peanut Butter Banana Protein Bars

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 1 very ripe banana, mashed
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1/2 cup vanilla protein powder
- 1/4 cup unsweetened flaked coconut
- 2 tbsp flaxseed meal
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips
- Water, as needed

## Instructions

1. Lightly spray an 8 by 8 inch pan or line it with parchment paper.
2. Microwave 1/2 cup peanut butter, 1/4 cup maple syrup, and 1 mashed ripe banana together for about 1 minute, just until softened and easy to stir smooth.
3. In a separate bowl, combine 2 cups rolled oats, 1/2 cup vanilla protein powder, 1/4 cup flaked coconut, 2 tbsp flaxseed meal, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, and 1/4 tsp sea salt.
4. Stir the oat mixture into the peanut butter mixture until well combined, adding a couple tablespoons of water if it seems too dry to hold together.
5. Stir in 1/2 cup chocolate chips.
6. Press the mixture into the prepared pan and refrigerate it until firm.
7. Lift the chilled slab out of the pan and cut it into bars.

## Serve With

- Breakfast or a quick snack.

## Notes

- Dried fruit can replace the chocolate chips.
- Store at room temperature or refrigerated.
